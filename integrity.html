Privacy Policy for Almanacka

Last updated: 2026-04-02

This privacy policy describes how netflimparr handles information in the Almanacka app.

1. Collection of Information

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information externally.

All data created by the user in the app, such as:

  • daily notes
  • birthdays
  • imported iCalendar files
  • other personal notes

is stored only locally on the user's device.

2. Use of Information

Since all data is stored locally on the device, it is only used to:

  • display the user's notes
  • manage calendar features and reminders

No information is sent to any server or third party.

3. Third Parties

The app uses no third-party services that collect data, such as:

  • analytics tools
  • advertising services (ads)
  • external data storage services

4. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on the device, it is the user's responsibility to protect their device, for example by using a password or biometric security.

5. Storage and Deletion of Data

All data is stored locally on the device and can be deleted by:

  • removing content within the app
  • uninstalling the app

When the app is uninstalled, all data is permanently deleted.

6. Children

The app is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you may contact:

Email: netflimparr@gmail.com
