Help for Almanacka

Last updated: 2026-05-14

This help page describes how the Almanacka app by netflimparr works.

The app has four different buttons at the bottom, each leading to its own screen with functions described below.

Home

The app displays a monthly view where you can scroll down through the entire selected year in a classic desk calendar style. Each day has a daily note field where you can write what happened that day or add reminders for future dates. Everything written here is stored only locally on the device, meaning you own and control your data yourself. In the daily note field, you can add small notes you want to keep track of or use it as an archive of past events. You can also write notes for events that happened a long time ago if you want to document older memories.

In addition to daily notes, the view also displays:

  • Today's date
  • Day of the week
  • Public holidays and holidays
  • Week numbers
  • Name days
  • Birthdays
  • Information from imported iCalendar files

Search

Here you can search for: notes, birthdays,name days, holidays, theme days and your own imported calendars. Search results begin appearing after entering just two letters, making it easy to quickly find many matches. You can also choose what you want to search by checking the respective box in the Search in filtering.

Holidays

This view provides an overview of upcoming events and dates. Under settings you can choose whether you want to see both holidays and theme days.

  • Current week number
  • Today's name (Swedish name days)
  • The nearest public holidays and bank holidays (you choose how many days in advance you want to see here under settings)

Notifications

Here you will see a list of: birthdays and imported iCalendar calendars for the upcoming 1,7,30 or 354 days (which you select under settings). Notification badges are shown here when, for example, there are new items in the list that you have not seen before, such as an upcoming birthday appearing 30 days in advance. You can also receive a device notification for birthdays on the actual day someone has a birthday.

The Menu on the Home Screen (...)

Below is a description of the different submenu options and their functions.

The menu contains the following submenu items:

  • Theme
  • Language
  • Push Notifications (toggle)
  • Alerts (toggle)
  • Privacy
  • Birthdays
  • Help
  • Settings
  • What's new
  • Rate the app

Theme

Here you can choose between light mode, dark mode, or system theme for the app's colors and appearance.

Language

The menu selection where you can switch languages ​​between Swedish and English.

Push Notifications (toggle)

Here you can enable or disable push notifications for birthdays, which are sent once per day. The notification time can be configured in Settings.

Notification Badges (toggle)

Here you can enable or disable the notification badges shown on the Notifications menu at the bottom.

Privacy

A page explaining how data is stored and handled by the app.

Birthdays

This menu option opens the birthday list. Here you can add, edit, or remove birthdays.

Help

Displays this help page.

Settings

Here you can customize the app so it looks and behaves the way you want.

Available settings include:

Home screen

  • Moon phases
  • Flag days
  • Birthdays
  • Name days
  • Week numbers
  • Holidays
  • Daylight saving time / standard time
  • Imported calendars
  • Highlight current day

System settings

  • Show or hide the menu bar

Push notifications

  • Push notification time
  • Birthdays - Choose whether you want push notifications on or off
  • Holidays - Choose whether you want push notifications on or off
  • Imported calendars - Choose whether you want push notifications on or off

Import and export

  • Import and export (backup birthdays and daily notes to a .csv file)
  • Import iCalendar (.ics files) and view a list of completed calendar imports. You can also remove imported calendars individually.

What's new

Get an info box about what was last changed in the app.

Rate the App

Here you can leave a rating for the Almanacka app in the Google Play Store.
